IMPROVEMENT PRIORITY

Clearer ownership & handoffs

The challenge

Redesigning handoffs and establishing clear team responsibilities. Inconsistent workflows and limited visibility can make performance difficult to manage.

The approach

Establish a baseline, observe work at the front line, and prioritize improvements with the team. Pilot changes before a wider rollout.

Making change last

Assign process owners, build simple reporting routines, and review outcomes against the agreed baseline.

A closer look at service delivery capacity

Map the administrative load

Identify recurring coordination, approval, and reporting tasks. Understand which activities support delivery and which create unnecessary repetition.

Clarify each handoff

Agree what information must be complete, who receives it, and how an exception is resolved without creating another queue.

Protect delivery time

Review workload and priorities regularly so that improvements release usable capacity rather than filling the calendar with more administration.
